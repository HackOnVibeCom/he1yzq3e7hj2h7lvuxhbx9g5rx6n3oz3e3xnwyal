# Question Pack · 日常小纠结 / Everyday Dilemmas

**状态 / Status：MVP 双语题包 / MVP bilingual question pack**

同一房间按以下顺序答八题。中文和英文对应同一套题及答案。
Everyone in a room gets the same eight questions in this order, in either language.

### 1. 最后一口 · The last bite

你最爱吃的菜就剩一口了，朋友问：「这口我能吃吗？」

- 吃吧，都给你。
- 咱俩一人一半。
- 抱歉，这口我要自己留着。

A friend wants the last bite of your favorite food. What do you say?

- You can have the whole bite.
- Let’s split it.
- I’m saving it for myself.

### 2. 认错人了 · Wrong person

迎面走来个人，你手都挥一半了，突然发现认错人了。你怎么办？

- 默默把手放下。
- 假装在伸懒腰。
- 干脆将错就错，就当打个招呼。

You’re halfway through a wave when you realize it’s the wrong person. What do you do?

- Put my hand down.
- Pretend I’m stretching.
- Keep waving. Might as well say hi.

### 3. 几个标签页 · How many tabs?

你手机浏览器里现在开着多少个标签页？凭直觉猜一下就行。

How many tabs are open in your phone browser right now? A guess is fine.

### 4. 超能力分给谁 · Superpower swap

假如给你三个超能力，自己留一个，送朋友一个，送你的死对头一个，你怎么分？

You get three superpowers. Keep one, give one to a friend, and give one to your nemesis. Who gets what?

- 粘枕头就秒睡 / Fall asleep instantly
- 永远能精准地抛梗接梗 / Always land the punchline
- 每次都能点到全桌最好吃的那道菜 / Always order the best dish

### 5. 剧透 · Spoilers

看电影看到一半，朋友突然问你：「要我给你剧透结局吗？」

- 别说，一个字都别说。
- 先给点提示，我再想想。
- 快说快说，我最爱被剧透了。

You’re halfway through a movie. Your friend asks, “Want me to spoil the ending?”

- Don’t say a word.
- Give me a hint, then I’ll decide.
- Tell me everything. I love spoilers.

### 6. 多一个小时 · One extra hour

每天白送你一个小时。你想早上多睡一小时，还是晚上多玩一小时？

- 早上多睡一小时。
- 晚上多玩一小时。

You get a free hour every day. Would you rather sleep in for an hour or have an extra hour to yourself at night?

- Sleep in for an hour.
- Take the extra hour at night.

### 7. 哪件最像你 · Sounds like me

下面哪件事最像你会干的？

Which of these sounds most like you?

- 遇到喜欢的歌能单曲循环一整天。 / Play a song on repeat as soon as I like it.
- 去餐厅吃饭总忍不住偷看隔壁桌点了啥。 / Peek at other tables before I order.
- 路上看到每一只小狗都要跟它打招呼。 / Say hi to every cute dog I see.

### 8. 老是找不着 · Always looking for

有什么东西是你每次急用却死活找不到的？

写一样就行。想不到可以跳过。

What’s one thing you can never find when you need it?

Name one thing, or skip.

## 答题规则 / Answer formats

- Q1、Q2、Q5、Q7 三选一。Q6 二选一。 / Pick one of three in Q1, Q2, Q5, and Q7. Pick one of two in Q6.
- Q3 填非负整数，可以估。 / Q3 takes a non-negative whole number. Estimates are fine.
- Q4 是唯一的分配题，三个位置各用一次。 / Q4 is the only assignment question. Use each fate once.
- Q8 填一个简短答案，也可以跳过。 / Q8 accepts a short answer and can be skipped.
